Nick Drake - Bryter Later (1971)

Motorpsycho (on a radio program sometime in the nineties) introduced me to Nick Drake. (My brother scribbled down ‘Nitetrake’, or something, in our own linear notes of the tape(!) recording, I seem to remember.) They highlighted his most famous Pink Moon, but I think I’m even more smitten by this Bryter Later. Favourite track might be Hazey Jane II. Or is it Fly? Or Poor Boy? Maybe it is all of them… (‘…a recordcollection missing this artist is not a[ny] recordcollection…’)
